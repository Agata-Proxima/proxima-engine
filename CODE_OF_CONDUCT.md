# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Agata Proxima open-source community aims to be welcoming, respectful, practical, and focused on improving the software. Participation in this repository and its issue tracker is conditional on treating others with dignity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when opinions or technical approaches differ.
- Critique code, ideas, and claims rather than attacking people.
- Share reproducible details and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and other people's intellectual property.
- Make room for contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions focused, accessible, and useful to maintainers and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized abuse, impersonation, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, customer records, private logs, or unpatched security vulnerabilities in public discussions.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when reasonably necessary to protect community members and project operations. They should explain moderation decisions when it is safe and practical to do so.

## Reporting a concern

For conduct concerns, email [support@agataproxima.com](mailto:support@agataproxima.com) with the subject **Community conduct report**, or use the contact options on the [Agata Proxima GitHub organization](https://github.com/Agata-Proxima). Share only the information needed to explain the concern; do not include unrelated private data.

For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. Do not publish exploit details in a conduct report or public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, issues, pull requests, code reviews, and when someone is officially representing the project in community spaces. It also applies when public conduct materially disrupts the safety of the project community.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant community standard. Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact route above.
